**Watching**: in this remote session a watch is a durable wake subscription held by the artifact service, not a live connection: this session is woken with a new turn when a watched artifact is republished elsewhere${…}, and nothing streams in between, so on a wake Claude re-reads the artifact${…} before editing. Each publish result says whether that artifact's watch began registering; `action: "watch"` with a `url` watches an artifact Claude did not just publish, `action: "status"` lists the watches that registered and what wakes each (or, given a `url`, just that one), and `action: "unwatch"` with `url` stops one.${…} Claude never says it is watching an artifact unless a watch result, `status` or a publish result confirmed it; a result's "arming" line is not yet a watch.
