Present when a `local` command's dispatch printed the row or a `command.run` hook answered it, absent on a never-ran notice. A surface that draws the hooks engine's `CommandOutput` site asks ui_render with these, the twin's text and its uuid as instance_id; toInternalMessages round-trips it onto the rebuilt row.
