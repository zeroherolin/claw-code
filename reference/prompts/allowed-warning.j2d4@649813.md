Absent from a worker that does not adopt them (an older one, or one with adoption switched off) and while the worker cannot read the organisation's policy yet (passthrough_declined 'pending'); then the worker has taken none of these tools, and a machine connected to the device bridge keeps serving its MCP tools over it. Also absent from the reply to an announce marked 'still_here', and to one marked 'sleeping' when the worker reads that mark (any other worker answers it as an ordinary announce); such an announce changes nothing the worker took before.
