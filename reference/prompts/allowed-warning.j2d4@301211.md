@internal A user-initiated shell command. Input-only — sent by CCR clients that surface a dedicated terminal UI; never emitted on stdout. Acknowledged on arrival and, unless blank, too long or a duplicate, echoed at once as a `<bash-input>` replay row; then answered with exactly one more row: its output (a replay frame when run beside the conversation; a plain user message after its own turn when it joins the conversation, possibly followed by the model replying) or a `<bash-stderr>Not run: …</bash-stderr>` replay row saying why it was refused or dropped. A blank or too-long command gets only its Not run row; a duplicate gets nothing. Other rows can come between the echo and that row.
